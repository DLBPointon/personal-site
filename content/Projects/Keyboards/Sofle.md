This is my first ergo split board, I ordered it pre-built from ErgoMech. Specifically, I splashed out on this board [here](https://ergomech.store/shop/sofle-v2-enclose-case-16#attr=99,100,104,105,108,280). It worked great for about a day, I got in touch with the guys over there and there were a couple of issues that slipped through and they sent me a replacement board which was great. Customer service was 10/10.

The replacement took a while to arrive due to the shipping company, however, once it got here it is quite something. I swapped out the boards and the [Silent Red MX2](https://mechboards.co.uk/products/cherry-mx-silent-red?variant=41880305172685) switches for [Oil Black Kings](https://mechboards.co.uk/products/gateron-oil-king?_pos=1&_sid=801a8fb70&_ss=r), I swapped the switches because I just didn't like the mushier feel although I can't deny that they are *much* quieter than the kings.

Absolute beauty of a board thou
